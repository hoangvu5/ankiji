Make a free Anki account (to study on your phone too)

  1. Go to ankiweb.net and click Sign Up. Enter your email and a password, then confirm the email they send you.
  2. Install Anki on your computer from apps.ankiweb.net and open it.
  3. Click Sync (top right of the main Anki window) and log in with the same email and password.
  4. On your phone install AnkiDroid (Android, free) or AnkiMobile (iPhone), log in with the same account and tap sync.
After adding cards from this app, click Sync in Anki on the computer, then sync on the phone, and the new cards appear there.

Connect Anki (do this first)

  1. Install Anki from apps.ankiweb.net if you don't have it, and open it.
  2. In Anki click Tools, then Add-ons, then Get Add-ons...
  3. Type this code and click OK:
    2055492159
  4. Close Anki and open it again.
  5. Come back here and click Recheck at the top. It should say Anki connected in green.
Keep Anki open whenever you use this app.

Set up the card type (fields, look and style)

A "note type" decides which fields a card has and how it looks. These ready-made ones have three fields: Front (the word with its reading), Back (the meaning) and Media (example sentence with image and audio), and make two cards per word: word to meaning, and meaning to word.

Easy way: one click

  1. Make sure Anki is open and the top of this app says Anki connected.
  2. Click the one you want:
  3. On the Make cards tab, in 3 Destination, choose it as the Note type (Japanese Card Format or Mandarin Card Format). Also pick the matching prompt in 2 Prompt.
Japanese: card 2 lets you handwrite the kanji on screen and checks it. Mandarin: card 2 asks you to recall the characters (handwriting checking only knows Japanese kanji). Example sentences from Nadeshiko are Japanese only, so the Mandarin Media field stays empty unless you add something yourself.

By hand (if you prefer, or to see what it does)

  1. In Anki click Tools, then Manage Note Types, then Add.
  2. Choose Add: Basic (and reversed card), click OK, and name it, for example Japanese Card Format.
  3. With it selected, click Fields..., then Add, type Media, click OK and close. You now have Front, Back and Media.
  4. Click Cards.... At the top, Card 1 is selected. Choose Front Template, delete what's there and paste the Card 1 front below. Do the same for Back Template and Styling.
  5. Switch the top dropdown to Card 2 and paste its front and back (styling is shared). Click Save.
  6. Japanese only: the handwriting card needs one extra file. Download _kanjicanvas.js, then press Windows key + R, paste %APPDATA%\Anki2 and press Enter. Open your profile folder (usually User 1), then collection.media, and move the file in there.
Want it different? You can ask an AI (ChatGPT or Claude) to change these templates for you: paste a template and say what you want, for example "make the word bigger and add a dark mode", "show the meaning in Vietnamese first", or "adapt this for Korean". Then paste the result back into Anki.

OpenAI API (easiest)

  1. Go to platform.openai.com and sign up or log in. This is separate from a ChatGPT subscription.
  2. Add a little money: click the gear icon (Settings), then Billing, then Add payment details. $5 lasts a long time for flashcards.
  3. Optional but smart: in Settings, open Limits and set a monthly budget so you can never spend more than you want.
  4. Open platform.openai.com/api-keys and click Create new secret key. Give it any name and click Create.
  5. Click Copy. The key starts with sk-. You can only see it once, so paste it in the next step right away.
  6. In this app click AI settings (top right), paste the key into API key, then click Save AI settings.
  7. On the Make cards tab, set AI to OpenAI API. It should say API key set.

ChatGPT subscription (via Codex)

Needs a paid ChatGPT plan (Plus or higher).
  1. Install Node.js: go to nodejs.org, download the LTS version, open it and click Next until it's done. Keep all the default options.
  2. Open Command Prompt: press the Windows key, type cmd, press Enter.
  3. Paste this and press Enter (wait until it finishes, about a minute):
    npm install -g @openai/codex
  4. Then paste this and press Enter:
    codex login
    A browser window opens. Choose Sign in with ChatGPT and log in with your ChatGPT account.
  5. When it says you're signed in, close the Command Prompt.
  6. On the Make cards tab, set AI to ChatGPT subscription and click Check again. It should say Ready.
"npm is not recognized"? Close the Command Prompt and open a new one after installing Node.js. If it still fails, restart the computer.

Claude subscription (via Claude Code)

Needs a paid Claude plan (Pro or Max).
  1. Install Git for Windows: go to git-scm.com, click Download for Windows, open it and click Next until it's done. Keep all the default options.
  2. Open PowerShell: press the Windows key, type powershell, press Enter.
  3. Paste this and press Enter:
    irm https://claude.ai/install.ps1 | iex
  4. Close PowerShell, open a new one, then paste this and press Enter:
    claude
    Follow the questions. When asked how to log in, choose your Claude account (subscription) and log in in the browser.
  5. When you see Claude's chat screen, type /exit and press Enter.
  6. On the Make cards tab, set AI to Claude subscription and click Check again. It should say Ready.

Nadeshiko (example sentences with image and audio)

  1. Make a free account at nadeshiko.co, then open nadeshiko.co/user/developer and create an API key. Copy it.
  2. In this app open the Nadeshiko tab and paste the key into Nadeshiko API key.
  3. Optional: paste your MyAnimeList profile link (your list must be public) and click Update from MyAnimeList. Then sentences come from anime you know first.
  4. After making cards, click Fill media above the review table. Each card gets a screenshot, the audio, and the Japanese and English subtitles.
Your note type needs a field for it, for example one called Media. The have it. Images and audio only go in with Straight into Anki.

then run claude once and log in with your Claude account. Then click "Check again" next to the AI dropdown.
